# Historical Streamlit scaffold

`streamlit_app.py` is an early interface scaffold. It does not load a final trained surrogate: its displayed hydraulic power uses an explicitly illustrative **3,000 Pa pressure drop**. The sliders include heat loads, geometries and flow ranges beyond the final study's validated domain; they do not establish prediction capability over those inputs.

Use the [current study](../README.md) and [evidence index](../docs/portfolio/EVIDENCE_AND_REPRODUCIBILITY.md) for the supported final results. The final configuration is 100 W, 25°C inlet and ten 0.30 × 0.70 mm channels; the earlier baseline used 0.50 × 0.50 mm channels. See the [history index](../docs/history/README.md) for the retained starter material.
