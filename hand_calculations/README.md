# Historical baseline hand calculations

`baseline_calculations.py` and `baseline_results.json` retain first-order estimates for the early **0.50 × 0.50 mm square-channel baseline**, using 100 W, 0.20 L/min total flow and 25°C inlet. They are useful for understanding heat flux, coolant rise and hydraulic scaling, but are not the final 0.30 × 0.70 mm COMSOL results or a substitute for their numerical checks.

See the [current study](../README.md), [final confirmed designs](../results/final_engineering_results/README.md) and [history index](../docs/history/README.md).
